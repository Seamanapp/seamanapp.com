Tasks that ramp before a port, personal and overtime blocks, and reminders that fire even offline. Rest hours are checked against MLC / STCW limits in the background — so if an inspector asks, the official record is one tap away.

Your career, on the record

Never miss a renewal. Never miss a promotion.

Your documents and sea time, structured the way promotions are actually judged.

Certificate vault & expiry radar

Every COC, GMDSS and medical with its scan attached — and a nudge 60, 30 and 7 days out, even with the app closed, even offline. Nothing lapses on your watch.

Rank readiness

Pick your target rank and the checklist lights up — what you hold, what's expiring, what's missing, your sea time, and which flags recognise your ticket. Then export a recruiter-ready CV.

Sea service, structured
